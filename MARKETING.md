# Never miss a game — watch what your TV doesn’t carry

## Stream licensed live games and highlights on any screen

This app brings games you can’t see on broadcast TV to your devices with a clean, distraction-free player, reliable low-latency streams, and simple tools to cast, record, and share highlights. Curated schedules, rights-cleared broadcasts, and robust privacy controls make it easy and safe to catch every play—wherever you are.

## Key features
- Live & on-demand: Watch licensed live games, condensed replays, and extended highlights.
- Cast & native TV apps: Native apps for smart TVs plus Chromecast and AirPlay support to put games on the big screen.
- Low-latency & DVR: Near-real-time streams for live action, plus cloud DVR and rewind during events.
- Personalized schedules: Follow teams and leagues, get push alerts for start times, score updates, and big plays.
- Multi-device continuity: Start on phone, continue on tablet or TV—playback resumes instantly.
- Data-saver & offline clips: Low-bandwidth mode and downloadable highlights for travel.
- Privacy & compliance: Clear licensing statements, optional anonymized usage, and easy data controls.
- Community features: Verified creator channels, moderated chat during games, and replay tagging.

## UX highlights
- One-tap “Follow” to add teams and auto-populate your watchlist and alerts.
- Clean player UI with persistent score overlay, timeline of key moments, and chapter markers for highlights.
- Quick access “Catch Up” page with last 48 hours of games and best plays.
- Lightweight onboarding: guest browsing, minimal permissions, and clear rights/credits for each stream.

## Technical strengths
- Low-latency delivery: Optimized HLS/DASH configurations and edge-enabled CDNs for minimal lag.
- Scalable streaming platform: Microservices with autoscaling to handle peak event loads.
- Secure playback: Tokenized stream URLs and DRM where required by rights holders.
- Observability & QoE: Real-user monitoring and automated alerts to maintain smooth playback during major events.
- Cross-platform CI/CD: Fast, tested releases across web, mobile, and TV apps.
